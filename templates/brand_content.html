Explainer 04 — Acoustic Steganography Explainer
~720 chars Hook: Analogy Bridge
Hook Pattern — Analogy Bridge → Technical Proof → So What
Establish a familiar analogy (hiding text in an image), then show how the VOID approach is both more sophisticated and more resilient.

You've heard of hiding text in an image — LSB steganography, the pixel trick. One bit per pixel, quiet and small.

PROJECT VOID's acoustic engine operates at a different scale entirely.

The carrier is biophony: 1,000 synchronised acoustic agents across three frequency shelves. Low-shelf whales (15–50 Hz) carry the heavy payload blocks. Mid-shelf birds (300–800 Hz) provide rhythmic parity triggers. High-shelf insects (2–12 kHz) provide the dense masking noise floor where LSB2 compressed data is buried.

The result: 378 MB of payload in one stereo hour. Encrypted with ChaCha20. Compressed with dual zlib/lzma auto-selection. Positions locked to a `.chirpmap.npy` sidecar that functions as a deterministic GPS for the decoder.

To a casual listener — or an automated content classifier — it sounds like badly recorded ambient audio. That's the point.

Sovereignty isn't about being loud. It's about being undetectable until you choose to be found. ◆

~640 chars Hook: Contrast / Myth-Bust
Hook Pattern — Contrast / Myth-Bust → Mechanism → Outcome
Open by rejecting what PEACE coin is not (speculation token), then precisely explain what it is and how it works.

PEACE coin is not a memecoin. It is not a governance token in the DeFi sense. It is not speculative.

PEACE coin is the economic immune system of the GriDul mesh.

Here is the mechanic: every node in the GriDul network generates a health signal — computation contributed, resources shared, protocol adherence verified by the Beehive consensus layer. It's not staking. It's not yield farming. It's closer to a mycorrhizal network — nutrients move to where growth is happening, not to where capital is concentrated.

VTX (Vortex Token) is the unit of computation — earned through work. PEACE is the signal of ecosystem health. They operate in parallel, not competition.

The economy of PROJECT VOID doesn't need a market cap. It needs a mesh. ◆
