Hardwood produces the warmest tone and longest spin. Brass gives the most resonant hum from the outer holes. Acrylic produces higher-pitched tones from the inner ring. Keep hole edges smooth — a burr will dampen the tone. Sand or file all drilled holes flush.

◆ How to Spin — Phirki Method
1

Thread the string. Loop the silk string through both centre holes so the disc hangs at the midpoint of the loop. Tie the loop ends together behind each hand — one end per hand.

2

Wind up. Hold both ends of the string taut and swing the disc in circles (like a jump rope) 8–12 times to twist the string. The disc should be visibly twisted at rest.

3

Pull to spin. With arms slightly extended, pull your hands apart sharply. The string unwinds and the disc accelerates. Do not let the string go fully slack.

4

Release and pull again. As the disc re-winds in the opposite direction, relax your hands slightly, then pull again at the moment of maximum re-wind. This pull — release — pull rhythm maintains rotation indefinitely.

5

Listen. At low RPM you will hear only the mid ring. Past ~400 RPM the outer ring begins to hum. At high RPM (700+) all three rings sing simultaneously — a convergent tone unique to each disc material.
