01 The Science

Wi-Fi CSI Biomechanics for Human Motion Sensing

Channel State Information (CSI) describes how a Wi-Fi signal is transformed as it propagates from transmitter to receiver. Every human body interacting with a radio environment creates predictable disturbances: amplitude variance rises when mass moves through the Fresnel zone, and phase shift patterns encode the geometry of that mass. PROJECT VOID's ESP32-S3 mesh nodes already capture these disturbances at 64 sub-channels to monitor mycelium growth in the machine enclosure. QiSync extends the same sensing layer to classify the human practitioner sharing that space.

Because Chinese martial arts foundation stances differ substantially in centre-of-mass height, lateral spread, and dynamic micro-motion, each generates a statistically distinct CSI signature. A wide low stance produces high amplitude variance across all sub-channels from the large body volume near ground level. An empty-step stance with most weight on one leg produces very low variance and tight phase coherence. These differences are large enough to discriminate reliably using variance-threshold classifiers — no machine learning required at this stage.

The 30-Chew Mastication Protocol

Research published in the Journal of the Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics (2011, Zhu et al.) and replicated across multiple cohorts demonstrates that thorough chewing — approximately 30 cycles per food bolus — produces measurable downstream effects on metabolic signalling: reduced ghrelin spike post-meal, elevated GLP-1 release (a satiety peptide), and a 10–12% improvement in protein absorption efficiency. The mechanism is mechanical: thorough mastication increases bolus surface area, reduces gastric transit time, and allows amylase full contact duration with complex carbohydrates before swallowing.

Wi-Fi CSI can detect jaw motion through micro-variance modulation. The masseter and temporalis muscles are among the densest in the body relative to their volume; their rhythmic contraction creates a reproducible low-amplitude oscillation in the Fresnel zone near the face. QiSync's MasticationDetector monitors zero-crossing frequency in the CSI amplitude stream to count cycles and score thoroughness.

VOID Metabolism Score

The metabolism score is a composite of two signals:

Stance Score (0–1) — weighted by hold duration and CSI confidence. Each second of a stable, correctly-classified stance increments the score; micro-motion above a threshold degrades it, rewarding genuine stillness over approximation.

Mastication Score (0–1) — ratio of completed chew cycles to the 30-cycle target. At 30 chews the masticatio score reaches 1.0. Below 15 the score degrades non-linearly, reflecting the steeper metabolic benefit loss at very low thoroughness.

Final metabolism score = 0.65 × stance_score + 0.35 × masticatio_score. This weighting reflects the larger metabolic footprint of sustained postural load vs. a single mastication bout. VOID credits are minted from this score at session end using the same ledger chain that records all sovereignty-layer transactions.
