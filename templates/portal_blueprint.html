This instrument scales that principle to the electromagnetic domain.

Grade N52, 30–50mm diameter. Spherical for uniform field distribution. High field strength ensures the rotating field writes clearly into the surrounding medium.

4–8 coils at equal intervals. H-bridge MOSFET driver. Microcontroller sequencing — Arduino or STM32. Firing sequence = orbital frequency. Variable speed via PWM.

AD9833 or equivalent. 0.1 Hz resolution minimum. Sweep mode enabled to scan from 7 Hz to 500 Hz. Amplitude control. SPI interface for microcontroller tuning.

4–8 sensors at nodal points. Continuous field intensity logging. Detects the differential between interior and exterior field conditions. Resonance lock visible in sensor data.

High contrast. Black ferrofluid on white dish for maximum visibility. Record the moment of resonance lock — the geometry stabilising suddenly. This is the evidence.
