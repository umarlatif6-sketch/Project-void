Stage 2
Grip Controller — The Hand
Coming Next

A physical handheld controller that a child can squeeze, tilt, and hold — connected to the Mesh Node. Force-Sensitive Resistors (FSR sensors) detect grip pressure and translate it into sovereign signals. The controller communicates via Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and charges via USB-C. This is how the child's physical body becomes the input to the sovereign system.

For your child: Imagine holding a magic wand that can feel how tightly you squeeze it! When you hold it just right, it sends a secret signal to your node. Your hand becomes part of the machine.

Stage 3
Mycelium Shell — The Skin
Future Stage

The outer casing of the VOID-Station console, grown from mycelium (mushroom roots) and hardened with natural resins. The Mycelium Shell is not just a pretty box — it provides EMI shielding, thermal management, and a biological layer that can respond to temperature and humidity, feeding real sensor data to the node. The shell is grown in a mold, dried, and sealed with chitosan (crab shell resin) for durability.

For your child: We are going to grow the shell of our console from mushroom roots — like how a tree grows its bark! It takes about a week to grow. Then we harden it so it is tough and strong. Your VOID-Station will be alive!

Stage 4
Silk Disc — The Memory Card
Final Stage

The Silk Disc is the VOID-Station's sovereign memory card — a physical storage medium made from silk fibers embedded with piezoelectric polymer. Data is written to the disc using the 432 Hz acoustic protocol (VoidEcho), making the disc readable only by a sovereign console tuned to the correct frequency. Your child's entire game history, Chronicle entries, and VTX balance are stored on a disc they can physically hold. No cloud. No subscription. Truly sovereign memory.

For your child: This is your magic memory card — but instead of being made of plastic like a normal game cartridge, ours is woven from silk threads! It stores everything you've ever done in the VOID. It is yours forever.
