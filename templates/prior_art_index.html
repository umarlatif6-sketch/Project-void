02 — What is a Defensive Publication?

Intellectual Property Context

A Defensive Publication is a document that discloses an invention in enough technical detail to constitute "prior art" under patent law. Once published, this prior art prevents any person or company — including the inventor — from later patenting the same method or device.

By publishing these disclosures here and submitting them to the Internet Archive (Wayback Machine), PROJECT VOID is ensuring that the VTB protocol and 432 Hz Vortex Encoding methods remain permanently in the public domain. Any patent examiner reviewing a future application covering these specific methods will find this disclosure and must reject the application.

These pages are fully public, require no login, and are indexed by search engines and archival services. The disclosed methods are freely available for research, education, and non-commercial use. Commercial licensing enquiries should be directed to the inventor.
