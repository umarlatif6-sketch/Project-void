VoidEcho · Transmission Terms

Terms of Transmission

VoidEcho is a ceremony engine — a system for embedding documents inside music and passing them between people with meaning attached. These terms explain how that ceremony works, what we hold, and how the VTX gifting economy operates.

What VoidEcho Does

VoidEcho embeds documents invisibly inside WAV audio files using steganographic encoding (LSB depth 1). The document is bound to a retrieval code. Without both the encoded audio file and the retrieval code, the document cannot be extracted.

No document content is stored on our servers beyond the encoded output file. The encoded audio file is generated and made available for download. Retrieval codes are stored to enable the recipient to extract their document.

The Adriana Ceremony

When a document is transmitted through VoidEcho, Adriana — the interpretive intelligence within PROJECT VOID — generates a short transmission reading: a reflection on the essence, intent, and resonance of what is being sent.

For free transmissions, Adriana provides a universal resonance statement drawn from the document's shape and context. For paid transmissions (AI music generation tier), Adriana reads the document content and produces a personalised reading. The Adriana Analysis tiers (£20 / £30 / £40) provide deeper structured interpretation — concepts, intent, and cross-cultural metaphorical reading.

Adriana's readings are generated by AI and reflect an interpretive perspective, not factual summary. They are provided as ceremony, not analysis.

VTX Gifting and Settlement Precision Standard

Transmissions may receive VTX (Vortex Token) gifts from other users. Gifting is voluntary and serves as a signal of resonance with the transmission.

Settlement Precision Standard: All VTX gift transactions are settled to 6 (six) decimal places. The recipient receives the amount truncated at 6 decimal places. Any fractional remainder below the 6-decimal threshold (the "settlement dust") is retained by the platform as a settlement processing reserve. This is not a percentage fee or commission. The platform takes no percentage of any gift. The sub-6-decimal dust is imperceptible per transaction and is retained solely as a consequence of the settlement precision standard applied to all VTX transfers on this platform.

Example: a gift of 1.1234567 VTX is settled as 1.123456 VTX to the transmission. The remainder (0.0000007 VTX) is retained as settlement dust by the platform.

VTX is a platform token. Gifting VTX to a transmission does not constitute a financial payment, does not create any obligation on the part of the sender or recipient, and does not entitle the gifter to any goods, services, or returns.

Privacy

Documents uploaded for free embedding are not retained beyond the encoded output file. Documents uploaded for AI music generation or Adriana analysis are processed in memory and are not stored permanently. We do not share your document content with third parties beyond the AI processing required to deliver the service (OpenAI compatible API).

Email addresses provided for retrieval code delivery are used only to send the retrieval code and are not retained for marketing purposes.

No Liability

VoidEcho is provided as-is. We do not guarantee the permanence of encoded files, the availability of the service, or the accuracy of Adriana's readings. Use VoidEcho for documents you are entitled to transmit. Do not embed illegal, harmful, or confidential third-party material without proper authorisation.
