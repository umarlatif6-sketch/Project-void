Questions
Can the recipient read the message without an account?
Yes. Anyone can visit the Reveal page, upload the audio file, and enter the retrieval code. No account or subscription required to receive a message.
Is the message encrypted or just hidden?
Both. The message is encrypted with ChaCha20 before it is embedded in the audio. Even if someone knows a message exists, they cannot read it without the retrieval code.
What audio files can I use as carriers?
Any WAV file. Music, ambient sound, voice recordings. The message hides inside the least significant bits of the audio — inaudible to any listener, invisible to any scanner.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel through your card provider or email us. No lock-in.
