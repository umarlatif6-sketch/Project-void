## SYNTHESIS PROCEDURE FOR ACCIO TO REFERENCE

### General Synthesis Steps (for all compounds)

**Step 1: Material Preparation**
- Source high-purity elements (99.99% minimum)
- Dry in vacuum oven (remove moisture)
- Prepare in powder form (high surface area)
- Weigh to exact stoichiometry (±0.1% accuracy)

**Step 2: Frequency Activation**
- Place powder in grinding chamber
- Apply 432 Hz frequency vibration
- Grind for 2-5 hours (compound-dependent)
- Monitor temperature (keep <100°C)
- Use inert atmosphere (nitrogen or argon)
- Maintain frequency stability (±0.1 Hz)

**Step 3: Dual-Polarity Alignment**
- Transfer ground powder to pressure vessel
- Apply pressure (800-3000 bar, compound-dependent)
- Maintain 432 Hz frequency during pressurization
- Cool to target temperature
- Hold for 1-2 hours (stabilization)

**Step 4: Consolidation**
- Release pressure slowly (prevent cracking)
- Cool to room temperature
- Anneal at 200-400°C (crystal structure optimization)
- Cool to room temperature
- Result: Solid compound ready for machining

**Step 5: Machining & Finishing**
- Machine to final shape using diamond tools
- Polish to required surface finish
- Inspect for defects (X-ray, ultrasonic)
- Apply protective coatings if needed
- Final testing and certification
