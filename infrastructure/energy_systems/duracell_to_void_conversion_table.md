# Duracell-to-VOID Conversion Table (Node Planning)

This table is an operational planning baseline for mesh-node economics.

| Input Stream | Goliath Outcome | VOID Outcome | Value Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spent battery unit | Discarded as waste | Routed to Ion-Resurrection scan lane | Converts stranded inventory into recoverable energy asset |
| Unknown chemistry batch | Mixed disposal | Segregate by chemistry and safety profile first | Reduces hazard and improves recovery predictability |
| High drift sample | Forced replacement | Controlled resurrection profile with safety gate | Captures circular margin while preserving auditability |
| Stable sample | New purchase dependency | Return to maintenance/charge lane | Extends cycle life and lowers procurement volatility |

## Unit-Economics Heuristic

- Capture cost target: near-zero via mesh collection routes.
- Recovery lane cost: dominated by QA and controlled diagnostics.
- Restored asset value: station-specific and chemistry-specific; benchmark using local replacement prices.

## Governance

- Every conversion decision should be logged in RFQ and energy audit records.
- Unsafe or out-of-bounds samples fail closed to disposal protocol.
