# Worm Grounding Protocol (Bio-Processor Lane)

This protocol treats worm activity as a biological grounding signal for the mesh ecosystem.

## Core Role

- Convert organic waste into nutrient-dense castings for aquaponics and soil recovery.
- Act as an early biological anomaly lane when toxins or vibration stress increase.
- Support silk and water loops by stabilizing local growth inputs.

## Monitoring Signals

1. Activity index
- Movement density and surface emergence frequency.

2. Survival continuity
- Weekly continuity ratio and reproduction trend.

3. Soil response
- Moisture retention and casting quality.

4. Vibration sensitivity
- Sudden movement suppression can indicate environmental stress.

## Integration with Project VOID

- Eye lane: compare worm activity trends with local climate and hydrology observations.
- Hand lane: route clean organic inputs from silk operations into bio-processing.
- Ground lane: fail closed if contamination thresholds are exceeded.

## Safety Boundaries

- No toxic feedstock.
- No heavy-metal contaminated substrate.
- Maintain stable moisture and oxygenation.

## Output

- Bio-liquidity score for node operations.
- Grounding status used by station operators for environmental calibration.
