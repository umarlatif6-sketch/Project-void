# Hangzhou-Standard Inspection Checklist

Use this checklist on every Node-HGH shipment before transfer to Node-SZN.

1. Tensile Symmetry
- Verify tensile hold under high-velocity winding and release cycles.
- Reject if spool drift exceeds tolerance in conductive_thread_specs.json.

2. Conductivity Lock Readiness
- Confirm substrate cleanliness for uniform Ag/Zn deposition.
- Reject if any pre-coating contaminants are detected.

3. Luster and Optical Resonance Surface
- Validate reflective profile for badge optical requirements.
- Reject if luster falls outside approved angle envelope.

4. Residue Redaction
- Verify zero lead and zero nickel residue.
- Verify no synthetic binder contamination.

5. Lot Provenance
- Capture batch, loom, and operator identifiers.
- Attach signed handoff packet for Node-SZN.
