KEEP INTENT INTACT

Hook:
Keep intent intact, do not let the signal crack,
In the noise, in the lapse, we still find the path back.
Not another voice that fades when the context slips,
We are building memory rails for the human grip.

Verse:
I seen tools talk fast and forget what I meant,
Bright replies, weak spine, no architectural intent.
Session cuts, thread breaks, every checkpoint gone,
Then they ask me start again like I never moved on.

Bridge:
The bottleneck is no longer intelligence.
The bottleneck is coherence.
If intent dies between steps, every system is expensive.
So we built a layer that remembers what matters.

Outro:
Keep the thread.
Keep the author.
Keep the why.
Keep intent intact.
