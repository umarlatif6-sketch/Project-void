Commission-based businesses have a fundamental challenge: **cash flow timing**. Money doesn't arrive instantly; it flows through multiple stages.

### Finance Model: The Commission Pipeline

```
Customer Books → Payment Received → Commission Calculated → Payout Issued
    ↓                ↓                    ↓                    ↓
  Entry         Accumulation        Exchange            Release
```

3. **Seasonal Loyalty** — Annual holiday bookings (predictable flow)

6. **Easy Rebook** — Agent makes next booking effortless (loyalty)
