# PROJECT VOID — CYBERSECURITY WEDGE

## Positioning

Lead with cybersecurity first.

Do not open with the full philosophy, the entire stack, or every subsystem. Open with the clearest institutional pain:

How do we protect sensitive intelligence when cloud dependence, model drift, and conventional network visibility create structural exposure?

Project VOID answers that with a fail-closed packet protocol, signed agent communication, sector-level access control, provenance tracking, and covert transmission options.

## Core Claim

Project VOID operates below the usual AI application layer.

It is not just another model wrapper, chatbot, or workflow assistant. It is protected intelligence infrastructure designed to preserve origin, constrain agent behavior, and reduce exposure during communication and artifact exchange.

## The Buyer Problem

Cybersecurity buyers already understand these issues:

- Centralized systems create single points of failure.
- Encrypted cloud storage still creates surrender risk.
- Multi-agent systems drift without strict control boundaries.
- Audit trails often exist after the fact, not at the protocol level.
- Sensitive artifacts move through too many surfaces before governance is applied.

## The Wedge

Project VOID gives them a security-native architecture at the packet and coordination layer:

1. Signed packet manifests
- Ed25519 verification for trust at handoff.

2. Sector ACL enforcement
- Access control attached to manifest policy, not left to convention.

3. Replay and freshness windows
- Reduced risk of stale or replayed operational artifacts.

4. Append-only packet audit logs
- Artifact resolution becomes provable and reviewable.

5. Steganographic transport option
- High-sensitivity communication can move through non-obvious acoustic carriers.
