## Card 1 — "Abstract Math" Challenge
**Pressure question:** Is Al-Jabr 286 a new math formula or just repackaged standard hashing?

**Claim-safe answer:** Al-Jabr 286 is not asserted as abstract math in isolation. The protectable contribution is a computer-implemented verification behavior in a deterministic transport pipeline, where integrity checks are bound to frame mapping, embed/recover stages, and fail-closed verification outcomes under disturbed media conditions.

**Technical effect language:** Reduced undetected payload corruption across compression/noise paths due to architecture-coupled verification, not formula alone.

**Evidence anchors:** `void_engine/al_jabr_286.py`, `void_engine/z_axis_video.py`, `VOID_AI_PACKET.md`, `LICENSE`.

**Boundary to state openly:** If asked to patent pure mathematics, do not claim that.
