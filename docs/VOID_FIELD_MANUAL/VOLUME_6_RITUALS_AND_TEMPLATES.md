# Volume 6 - Rituals and Templates
## The Daily Operator Companion

Purpose:
- Give you reusable pages for meetings, strategy, and execution.

---

## Ritual 1 - Morning Signal Lock (10 min)

1. Read your category line once.
2. Read the day objective once.
3. Choose one measurable output.
4. Declare one risk likely to appear.

Seal:
- "Today I protect coherence before expansion."

---

## Ritual 2 - Midday Reset (5 min)

1. Stop all tabs.
2. Ask: are we proving or performing?
3. Remove one non-essential task.
4. Return to benchmark-critical work.

Seal:
- "Return to the loop."

---

## Ritual 3 - Evening Chronicle (12 min)

Write four lines:
1. Decision made.
2. Evidence observed.
3. Cost paid.
4. Next lock.

Seal line:
- "Memory recorded for tomorrow's precision."
