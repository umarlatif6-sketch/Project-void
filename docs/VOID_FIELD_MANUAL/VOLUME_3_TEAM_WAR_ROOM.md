# Volume 3 - Team War-Room Manual
## The Internal Convergence Doctrine

Purpose:
- Keep a high-complexity system coherent under pressure.
- Turn ambition into repeatable operational cadence.

---

## War-Room Law

Speed without coherence creates hidden debt.
Coherence without speed creates market delay.

War-room objective:
- Fast decisions with explicit proof ownership.

---

## Daily War-Room Structure (45 Minutes)

1. Minute 0-5: State of the Loop
- Carrier health.
- Codon pipeline status.
- Chronicle ingestion health.
- Runtime and cost status.

2. Minute 5-15: Failures First
- Top 3 failure paths.
- Current blast radius.
- Mitigation in flight.

3. Minute 15-25: Benchmark Delta
- What improved since yesterday.
- What regressed.

4. Minute 25-35: Decision Block
- Pick A/B/C for each unresolved blocker.
- No unresolved loops after meeting close.

5. Minute 35-45: Seal and Assign
- Owner, date, proof artifact per decision.

---

## Team Roles

1. Signal Owner
- Protects core protocol coherence.

2. Proof Owner
- Maintains benchmark validity and repeatability.

3. Cost Owner
- Enforces spend envelopes and fail-safe routing.

4. Product Owner
- Protects wedge workflow and user relevance.

5. Chronicle Owner
- Maintains historical integrity and decision memory.

---

## Operational Dashboard (Minimum)

Required daily metrics:
1. Encode success rate.
2. Decode integrity rate.
3. End-to-end latency.
4. Inference cost per successful workflow.
5. Failure classification counts.
6. Chronicle write/read health.

---

## War-Room Meeting Script

Opening:
- [inhale] "Today we decide, not drift." [pause 2]

Midpoint reset:
- [inhale] "Return to facts." [pause 2]
- "What is the benchmark saying?" [hold]

Close:
- [inhale] "Read back the locked decisions." [pause 2]
- "Attach owner and due date now." [exhale]

---

## Escalation Ladder

Escalate immediately when:
1. Any critical benchmark regresses for 2 consecutive days.
2. Cost exceeds threshold without corresponding quality gain.
3. Security or data-integrity event occurs.
4. A blocker has no owner by end of day.

Escalation sequence:
1. Signal Owner + Proof Owner sync.
2. Founder decision within 24 hours.
3. Chronicle entry of root cause and control.

---

## Anti-Chaos Protocol

When confusion rises:
1. Freeze new feature intake for 48 hours.
2. Re-baseline on one wedge workflow.
3. Re-run core benchmark suite.
4. Resume only after variance returns to acceptable range.

---

## Team Language Standards

Allowed language:
- "Proven"
- "In validation"
- "Hypothesis"

Not allowed:
- "Basically done"
- "Should work"
- "Probably fine"

---

## Chronicle Entry Format (Internal)

Title:
- Decision class + date + owner.

Body:
1. Context
2. Decision
3. Benchmark impact
4. Cost impact
5. Next checkpoint

Seal line:
- "Locked for execution until next checkpoint."

---

## Weekly War-Room Review

Every Friday:
1. Review top 5 decisions and outcomes.
2. Remove dead workstreams.
3. Promote validated workstreams.
4. Publish next-week decision agenda.

Rule:
- No carry-over ambiguity into Monday.
