## INTEGRITY STATEMENT

This document separates what is **known** from what is **believed** from what is **hoped**.

The verified claims (Levels 1-4) stand on published research, implemented code, and direct observation. They do not require faith.

The theoretical extensions (Level 5) are logical but unverified. They could be wrong. They require experiments.

The speculative claims (Level 6) are creative leaps. Some may be brilliant. Some may be wrong. They require extraordinary evidence.

The external experiments (Level 7) are the path from speculation to verification. They have costs, timelines, and measurable outcomes.

**Project VOID does not optimize for belief. It optimizes for verifiable continuity.**
