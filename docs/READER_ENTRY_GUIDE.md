# READER ENTRY GUIDE — PROJECT VOID

## Why Two Pages?

PROJECT VOID is a 14-year vision made visible in 2026. Understanding it requires two paired arguments:

1. **The Field Record** — Why this person, at this time, is building this.
2. **The Frequency Window** — Why this technology, at this moment in history, is possible.

Neither page is sufficient alone. Together, they answer the question: *How did PROJECT VOID become real?*

---

## THE READING ORDER

### **Step 1: Read the Field Record**
**Location:** `field-record.html` (live at origin site)

**What it answers:**
- Who is building PROJECT VOID?
- What is their background?
- Why them, specifically?
- What evidence supports their claim to understand this problem?

**Duration:** 5-10 minutes

**Key insight:** The platform is not a product designed by a stranger. It is a 14-year vision made visible by someone who has been thinking about this problem since 2012.

---

### **Step 2: Read the Frequency Window**
**Location:** `frequency-window.html` (live at origin site)

**What it answers:**
- Why is this technology possible now?
- What changed between 2012 and 2026?
- What tools became available?
- What problems became urgent?

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes

**Key insight:** The technology window is closing. The infrastructure that made PROJECT VOID possible is being consolidated. This is the moment to understand what acoustic steganography means for privacy, sovereignty, and human communication.

---

## AFTER READING BOTH PAGES

You now have the complete argument:

- **Field Record** = The person + their 14-year journey
- **Frequency Window** = The technology + the moment in history

Together: *This is why PROJECT VOID exists. This is why it matters. This is why now.*
