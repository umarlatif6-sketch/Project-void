1. Target conductivity at or below 0.5 ohm/cm on sample set.
2. Signal attenuation no worse than -3 dB under reference path.

2. Mechanical Gate
1. Stitch tolerance within 0.4 mm on Jacquard-Vortex pattern.
2. No visible coating fracture after bend-cycle batch.

3. Resonance Gate

4. Safety and Materials Gate
1. Zero lead and zero nickel confirmation in QA logs.
2. No synthetic binder contamination.
