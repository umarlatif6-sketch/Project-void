## 4. ASSEMBLY INSTRUCTIONS (STEP-BY-STEP)

### Phase 1: Foundation & Base (45 minutes)

**Step 1.1: Mount Base Frame**
1. Prepare level concrete surface (flatness ±3 mm over 3 m)
2. Position base frame (stainless steel, 1200 × 1200 mm)
3. Install 4 leveling feet (adjustable, vibration isolators)
4. Bolt frame to foundation (M16 studs, 4 anchor bolts)
5. Torque: 110 N·m (81 ft-lbs)
6. Verify level in all directions

**Step 1.2: Install Support Columns**
1. Mount left support column (vertical, 1800 mm)
2. Mount right support column (vertical, 1800 mm)
3. Connect cross-braces (horizontal stability)
4. Torque all bolts: 150 N·m (111 ft-lbs)
5. Verify vertical alignment (±5 mm over height)

**Tools Required:**
- Torque wrench (0-200 N·m)
- Socket set (metric)
- Level (precision, ±0.5 mm/m)
- Measuring tape

---

### Phase 2: Water Bubbler Tank Installation (30 minutes)

**Step 2.1: Position Tank**
1. Lower water bubbler tank onto base frame using lifting equipment
2. Align tank center with support columns
3. Install vibration isolation pads (elastomer, 20 mm thick)
4. Secure tank with 4 hold-down clamps

**Step 2.2: Install Tank Ports**
1. Install water inlet port (Ø50 mm, top center)
   - Apply thread sealant (PTFE tape, 5 wraps)
   - Torque: 110 N·m
2. Install pressure relief valve (Ø25 mm, top)
   - Set pressure: 10 bar (150 psi)
   - Torque: 80 N·m
3. Install exhaust gas inlet (Ø75 mm, side)
   - Position 300 mm above tank bottom
   - Tangential entry (45° angle)
   - Torque: 110 N·m
4. Install water outlet port (Ø50 mm, bottom center)
   - Torque: 110 N·m
5. Install drain valve (Ø25 mm, bottom)
   - Ball valve type
   - Torque: 80 N·m

**Step 2.3: Install Instrumentation**
1. Install pressure gauge (Ø15 mm, side)
   - Range: 0-16 bar
   - Snubber valve (to dampen fluctuations)
2. Install temperature gauge (Ø15 mm, side)
   - Range: 0-120°C
   - Thermowell (stainless steel)
3. Install water level sight glass (front)
   - Ball valves at top and bottom
   - Isolation capability

**Estimated Time:** 30 minutes
**Tools:** Socket set, torque wrench, thread sealant, lifting equipment

- [ ] Exhaust gas source ready (350-600°C)
- [ ] Safety equipment available (fire extinguisher, first aid)

2. Start water circulation pump (low speed, 5 m³/h)

### 6.2 Troubleshooting Guide

**Problem: Low O₂ Output Purity (< 85%)**

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Purity drops gradually | Water contamination | Drain tank, refill with clean water |
| Purity drops suddenly | Pump cavitation | Check water inlet valve, increase inlet pressure |
| Purity low at startup | Cold water temperature | Preheat water to 25-30°C |
| Purity inconsistent | Exhaust gas composition varies | Stabilize exhaust source, check for CO₂ leaks |

**Problem: High Water Temperature (> 70°C)**

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Temp rises continuously | Insufficient cooling | Increase water circulation rate |
| Temp spikes suddenly | Pump cavitation | Check pump inlet, verify water level |
| Temp high after startup | Cold water not preheated | Allow 30 min warm-up period |
| Temp won't stabilize | Heat exchanger fouled | Clean heat exchanger tubes |

**Problem: Pump Noise or Vibration**

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Cavitation noise (grinding) | Low inlet pressure | Check inlet valve, increase water level |
| Bearing noise (squealing) | Bearing wear | Lubricate bearings, replace if necessary |
| Vibration (rattling) | Loose bolts | Retorque all pump mounting bolts |
| Vibration (grinding) | Impeller damage | Replace pump impeller assembly |

**Problem: Leaks**

| Location | Cause | Solution |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Pipe connections | Loose fittings | Retorque connections (110 N·m) |
| Tank seams | Corrosion or cracking | Drain tank, weld repair, pressure test |
| Pump seal | Seal wear | Replace mechanical seal assembly |
| Valve stem | Packing wear | Tighten packing nut, replace if necessary |

### 6.3 Performance Monitoring

## 7. SAFETY PROCEDURES

### 7.1 Hazard Analysis

|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **High Temperature** | High | Thermal insulation, warning labels, PPE |
| **High Pressure** | High | Pressure relief valve, rupture disk, inspection |
| **Rotating Parts** | Medium | Guards, interlocks, training |
| **Electrical Shock** | Medium | Grounding, GFCI protection, lockout/tagout |
| **Water Contamination** | Low | Regular water testing, drain procedures |
| **Gas Leaks** | Low | Pressure test, leak detection, ventilation |

### 7.2 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

**Required PPE:**
- Safety glasses (impact-resistant)
- Heat-resistant gloves (for hot surfaces)
- Steel-toe boots (for heavy equipment)
- Hard hat (in industrial environment)
- High-visibility vest (if in traffic area)

**Optional PPE:**
- Face shield (when working with pressurized connections)
- Thermal protective apron (when near hot surfaces)
- Respirator (if working in confined space)

### 7.3 Emergency Procedures

**In Case of Pressure Vessel Rupture:**
1. Evacuate area immediately (50 m radius)
2. Call emergency services (911 or local equivalent)
3. Do NOT attempt to repair
4. Do NOT approach vessel
5. Allow system to cool before investigation

**In Case of Thermal Burn:**
1. Cool burned area with water (10-20 minutes)
2. Remove contaminated clothing
3. Cover burn with clean cloth
4. Seek medical attention immediately
5. Do NOT apply ice directly to burn

**In Case of Electrical Shock:**
1. Turn off electrical power immediately
2. Do NOT touch victim if still in contact with electricity
3. Call emergency services
4. Perform CPR if trained and necessary
5. Seek medical attention immediately

### 7.4 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) Procedure

**Before Maintenance:**
1. Turn off all electrical power (disconnect at breaker)
2. Install lockout hasp on electrical disconnect
3. Install "DO NOT OPERATE" tag on hasp
4. Verify power is OFF (test with multimeter)
5. Relieve all pressure (open drain valve slowly)
6. Allow system to cool (30 minutes minimum)
7. Proceed with maintenance

**After Maintenance:**
1. Verify all tools and equipment removed
2. Close all drain valves
3. Verify all connections tight
4. Remove LOTO tag and lock
5. Restore electrical power
6. Perform function check before operation

### Appendix C: Calculation Examples

**Example 1: System Efficiency Calculation**

Given:
- Exhaust gas inlet temperature: 450°C
- Exhaust gas outlet temperature: 80°C
- Water inlet temperature: 25°C
- Water outlet temperature: 55°C
- Exhaust gas flow rate: 5,000 Nm³/h
- Water flow rate: 15 m³/h

Calculation:
- Heat absorbed by water = m × cp × ΔT
- Heat absorbed = 15,000 kg/h × 4.18 kJ/kg·K × (55-25)K
- Heat absorbed = 1,881,000 kJ/h = 522 kWh/h

- Heat available in exhaust = m × cp × ΔT
- Heat available = (5,000 × 1.29 kg/h) × 1.01 kJ/kg·K × (450-80)K
- Heat available = 2,410,000 kJ/h = 669 kWh/h

- Efficiency = 522 / 669 = 78%
