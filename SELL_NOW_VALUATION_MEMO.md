# Sell-Now Valuation Memo

Date: 2026-05-19
Owner: Project VOID
Objective: Sell project as-is (asset sale, potential acqui-hire upside)

## 1) Valuation Positioning

### Base reality (as-is)
- Most likely transaction type: asset purchase, not full startup acquisition.
- Why: unclear recurring revenue and limited commercial proof currently visible.
- Strength: unusual depth of system architecture, active codebase, differentiated narrative and operator-control thesis.

### Recommended pricing bands
- Anchor ask price: $750,000
- Target close band: $300,000 to $600,000
- Hard floor (walk-away line): $180,000

### Why these numbers
- Below $180,000, buyer is effectively paying only for code salvage and optional acqui-hire.
- $300,000 to $600,000 is plausible for a strategic buyer who values speed-to-market plus founder transfer support.
- $750,000 anchor creates room to negotiate while signaling strategic value, not commodity repo value.

## 2) Deal Structures to Offer

Use deal options to keep price power while making signing easier.

### Option A: Cash asset purchase
- Purchase price: $300,000 to $600,000
- Includes: code, docs, brand assets, transfer support (30 to 45 days)

### Option B: Cash + earnout
- Upfront: $200,000 to $350,000
- Earnout: $200,000 to $800,000 tied to milestones (launch, customer, revenue)
- Use when buyer says "too early" but still wants strategic control.

### Option C: License-first with buyout
- License fee: $75,000 to $150,000 for 6 to 12 months
- Buyout option: fixed pre-agreed range $300,000 to $700,000
- Good for enterprise buyers who move slowly through procurement.

Focus on buyers where continuity/control-plane value is accretive, not confusing.

## 5) Negotiation Script

Use this script as the default sequence.

### Opening (position)
"This is not a generic AI wrapper. It is an acquisition-ready continuity/control-plane asset with transfer support and immediate integration utility. We are evaluating strategic-fit buyers in a defined timeline."

### If buyer pushes low (e.g., under $150k)
"At that level we would be pricing this as salvage code, which is not an accurate reflection of the transfer value. If strategic fit exists, we can discuss structure (cash + earnout), but we are not able to transact at salvage pricing."

### If buyer says "too early"
"That is exactly why this is accretive: you can buy roadmap compression now. We can de-risk with structure: lower upfront + milestone earnout."

### If buyer asks exclusivity early
"We can offer short exclusivity only after a signed LOI with clear economics and diligence timeline."

### Close prompt
"If strategic fit is real, next step is LOI this week with one of three structures: cash asset buy, cash + earnout, or license-first with buyout option. Which lane fits your process?"

## 6) Due Diligence Pack (Minimum)

Have this ready before outreach.

1. Product demo video (5 to 8 min)
2. Architecture summary (2 pages)
3. Feature/integration map
4. Repo transfer inventory
5. IP and license clarity statement
6. 30/60/90 integration plan for buyer teams
7. Founder transition scope (hours/week, duration)

## 7) Non-Negotiables

1. No perpetual unpaid advisory expectations post-close
2. No open-ended support without scoped SOW
3. No exclusivity before LOI economics
4. No transfer before funds and signed assignment
