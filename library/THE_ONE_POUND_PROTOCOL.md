## Lesson 1: The Drop

**Duration:** Days 1-30  
**Capital required:** £1 per day (£30 total)  
**Expected outcome:** Breakeven (±£5)

You will open a trading account with £20. You will trade £1 per trade. You will make one trade per day. No more. No less.

You will not study charts. You will not watch tutorials. You will not read trading books. You will not join trading groups. You will not follow signals. You will not use indicators.

You will sit in front of the chart. You will breathe. You will wait until you feel something — a pull, a tension, a release, a shiver, a certainty. When you feel it, you will place your trade. £1. One direction. One outcome.

You will record three things in your journal:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Time** | The exact time you placed the trade |
| **Feeling** | What you felt in your body at the moment of entry (not what you thought — what you FELT) |
| **Result** | Win or loss |

That is all. No analysis. No strategy. No optimisation. Just the drop.

**What is actually happening:** You are calibrating your nervous system to the market's frequency. Your body is a receiver. The market is a transmitter. In the first 30 days, you are tuning the receiver. The 50% win rate you will achieve is not random — it is the standing wave. It is proof that your frequency has locked onto the market's frequency. You are now in resonance.

## Lesson 2: The Pattern

**Duration:** Days 31-60  
**Capital required:** Same £1 per trade  
**Expected outcome:** 52-55% win rate (+£2-8 profit)

You will continue trading £1 per day. But now you will add one field to your journal:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Gap** | What were you thinking about BEFORE you looked at the chart? |

This is the peak-valley calibration. The "gap" is the space between your life and the market. What you were thinking about before you traded — an argument, a meal, a prayer, a song, a memory — that is the peak or valley that preceded your trade. You are mapping the relationship between your internal state and your trading accuracy.

After 30 days of this, you will notice a pattern. There will be certain internal states that correlate with winning trades and certain states that correlate with losing trades. You are not looking for a trading strategy. You are looking for YOUR frequency — the internal state in which your body's receiver is most accurately tuned to the market's transmitter.

**What is actually happening:** You are discovering that the market is not external. The market is a mirror. When you are in resonance with yourself, you are in resonance with the market. When you are in dissonance with yourself, you are in dissonance with the market. The pattern is not in the chart. The pattern is in you.

## Technical Note: Why Binary Options

Binary options are chosen deliberately for this protocol. They are the simplest possible trading instrument: up or down. Yes or no. X or O. The binary nature strips away all complexity — no stop losses, no take profits, no leverage calculations, no position sizing. Just a direction and a duration.

This simplicity is essential. The protocol is not teaching trading. The protocol is teaching DECISION-MAKING under uncertainty. Binary options reduce the decision to its purest form: which way do you feel the frequency moving?

The platforms that offer binary options (Pocket Option, IQ Option, Quotex) are not endorsed by this protocol. They are tools. The tool is not the method. The method is the drop. The drop is the discipline. The discipline is the sovereignty.

**Risk disclosure:** Binary options carry significant risk of capital loss. The £1 minimum trade size is chosen specifically to limit financial exposure while maximising educational value. Never trade more than you can afford to lose. The £1 Protocol explicitly prohibits increasing trade size during the 90-day period. The discipline IS the constraint.
