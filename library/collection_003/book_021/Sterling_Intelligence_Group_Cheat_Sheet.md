# Sterling Intelligence Group: Consultant's Cheat Sheet

This document provides a quick-reference guide for Faiz, covering the core value proposition, key questions, and simplified explanations for each of Sterling Intelligence Group's 7 divisions. Use this to build confidence and speak authoritatively with potential clients.

---

## 1. The Trade Growth Studio

*   **Industry Talk**: "Online visibility is the new word-of-mouth for trades. Are you dominating your local search results, or are you losing jobs to competitors with better digital profiles?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If a potential customer in Huddersfield searches for 'best plumber,' are you consistently the first and most trusted option they see?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We optimize your online profiles (Google, Checkatrade, Trustpilot) with persuasive language and a strategy to get more 5-star reviews, ensuring you attract high-value jobs.

---

## 2. Lexis-AI

*   **Industry Talk**: "In the legal sector, information is power, but data overload is a silent killer. How much billable time is your team losing sifting through documents that an AI could process in seconds?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If you needed to find every instance of a specific clause across 500 client contracts right now, how long would that take your team, and what's the cost of that delay?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We build a private, secure AI 'brain' for your firm that instantly searches, summarizes, and analyzes your internal legal documents, giving your team answers in seconds.

---

## 3. Sterling Cyber-Governance

*   **Industry Talk**: "The rise of AI tools in the workplace presents both massive opportunities and critical new security risks. Is your firm's data protected from 'shadow AI' usage, and are you compliant with evolving UK regulations?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If one of your staff accidentally uploaded sensitive client data to a public AI tool today, do you have a formal policy and a response plan in place to protect your firm and satisfy your insurers?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We audit your AI usage, draft robust AI governance policies, and implement a framework to protect your data, ensure compliance, and safeguard your firm's reputation from new digital threats.

---

## 4. Sterling Global Intelligence

*   **Industry Talk**: "Global supply chains are more fragile than ever. Is your business prepared for the next geopolitical shock, or will a single disruption halt your entire operation?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If your primary overseas supplier was cut off tomorrow due to unforeseen events, do you have a pre-vetted 'Plan B' ready to ensure your production never stops?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We use AI to map your entire supply chain, identify critical vulnerabilities, and proactively vet alternative suppliers, building a robust 'Plan B' to ensure your business continuity.

---

## 5. Sterling Estate Intelligence

*   **Industry Talk**: "In the competitive UK property market, finding the next million-pound development opportunity requires foresight, not just legwork. Are you leveraging data to find hidden gems before your competitors?"
*   **Killer Question**: "How many high-potential development sites are you missing because your team is manually sifting through planning portals, while competitors are using AI to find them first?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We use AI to scan council planning data and land registries, identifying undervalued plots with high development potential and providing you with a detailed feasibility blueprint.

---

## 6. Sterling Revenue Recovery

*   **Industry Talk**: "For service businesses with mobile teams, revenue leakage is a silent killer. Are you losing thousands every month to unbilled work, inefficient operations, or missed charges?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If I told you we could find tens of thousands of pounds in revenue you've already earned but haven't collected, would you be interested in seeing exactly where it is?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We use AI to audit your operational data (timesheets, GPS, invoices) to pinpoint and quantify lost revenue, then provide a clear plan to recover it and prevent future leaks.

---

## 7. Sterling Private Office

*   **Industry Talk**: "For high-net-worth individuals and CEOs, time is the ultimate luxury. Are you drowning in information, or do you have a strategic intelligence layer filtering the noise and amplifying your decision-making?"
*   **Killer Question**: "If you could gain back 20 hours a week and ensure every critical decision was backed by instant, unbiased intelligence, what would that be worth to you?"
*   **How it Works (Simple)**: We act as your private AI Chief of Staff, centralizing all your critical information, filtering the noise, and providing concise, actionable insights for your most important business and personal decisions.
