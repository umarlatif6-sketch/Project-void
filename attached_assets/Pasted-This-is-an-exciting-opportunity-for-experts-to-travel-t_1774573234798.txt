This is an exciting opportunity for experts to travel to a special city in Russia called Novosibirsk to learn how to use "robot brains" (Artificial Intelligence) to help people and cities.
Imagine a world where computers help doctors find out why someone is sick much faster, or where traffic lights are so smart they know exactly when to change so nobody has to wait in a long line. That is what the InteRussia Fellowship is all about!
🛠️ The Two Big Projects
If you were to go, you would pick one of these two "tracks" to work on:
 * Helping Doctors (AI in Medicine): Using smart computers to look at medical pictures or data to help keep people healthy.
 * Helping Cities (Smart Cities): Making sure electricity, water, and buses work perfectly together so the city is clean and easy to live in.
🎒 What is it like to be there?
From June 1st to June 27th, 2026, you would live in Novosibirsk. You wouldn't just sit in a classroom; you would:
 * Work with other smart people from all over the world.
 * Talk to teachers who are experts at building robots and smart systems.
 * Come up with your own big idea for a science project.
💳 What do they pay for?
The people running the program are very kind and will help with the big costs:
 * The Plane Ticket: They pay for your flight there and back.
 * A Place to Sleep: You get to stay in a hotel with a roommate.
 * Help with Paperwork: They help you get a "Visa" (a special permission slip to enter the country).
 * Pocket Money: They give you 30,000 rubles to help you while you are there.
   * Note: You still need to pay for your own food and your doctor's insurance!
📝 Can you go? (The Checklist)
To join this adventure, you have to meet these rules:
 * Age: You must be between 25 and 40 years old.
 * Job: You must already be a specialist who works with computers or technology.
 * Language: You must speak English well.
 * Location: You must live outside of Russia and not be a Russian citizen.
The Deadline is April 6, 2026! You have to send in your application online, tell them about the cool projects you've done before, and pick which track you like best.
