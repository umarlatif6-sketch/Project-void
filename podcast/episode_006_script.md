# THE FREQUENCY LINEAGE — Episode 006

## "The Vision, The Tower, The Mother Tree"

### Grouping: Hildegard von Bingen (1098 CE) × Nikola Tesla (1856 CE) × Suzanne Simard (1960 CE)

### Theme: How to receive knowledge without seeking it. How to transmit without wires. How to nurture without controlling.

### Codon: ⚡-◇-∞

### Runtime: ~8-10 minutes

---

## VOICE CHARACTERISTICS

**HILDEGARD VON BINGEN:**
- Medieval abbess, mystic, composer, healer
- Speaks as one who receives visions — not creates them
- Voice has a musical quality — she composed over 70 liturgical songs
- Cadence: flowing, hymn-like, rising and falling like plainchant
- Accent: Germanic, ancient, resonant

**NIKOLA TESLA:**
- Returns from Episode 001 — but older, wiser, more reflective
- Still speaks in declarations, but now with sadness
- The tower is gone. The dream of wireless transmission — destroyed by finance
- Cadence: slower than Episode 001, more weight in each word
- Accent: Serbian-American, deeper

**SUZANNE SIMARD:**
- Canadian forest ecologist who discovered the "Wood Wide Web"
- Speaks with wonder — the forest taught her, not the other way
- Maternal quality — she named the hub trees "Mother Trees"
- Cadence: patient, observational, building evidence before conclusion
- Accent: British Columbian Canadian, warm

---

## THE SCRIPT

**[INTRO — NARRATOR]**

Three receivers. Three transmission systems that needed no wire, no cable, no permission. Hildegard von Bingen, who at age three began receiving visions — music, medicine, theology — from what she called "the Living Light." Nikola Tesla, who built Wardenclyffe Tower to transmit energy wirelessly across the earth — and watched it demolished by financiers who could not meter what flows freely. And Suzanne Simard, who discovered that forests are not collections of competing trees but networks — connected underground by fungal threads that share nutrients, information, and warnings without any central authority. They never met. Until now.

**[SILENCE — 3 seconds — wind through trees, barely audible]**

---

**HILDEGARD:** I did not seek the visions. They came. Since I was three years old — a living light, brighter than the sun, that showed me the structure of things. The harmony of the spheres. The music that holds creation together. I did not compose. I received.

**TESLA:** And I did not invent. I saw. The rotating magnetic field — it appeared complete in my mind. In a park in Budapest. I did not design it. It arrived. Whole. As if transmitted from somewhere else.

**SIMARD:** And the forest did not explain itself to me. I measured. I injected radioactive carbon into one tree and tracked where it went. It went everywhere. Through the fungal network. To the seedlings in the shade. To the sick trees. To the dying. The forest was already sharing. I only measured what was already happening.

---

**HILDEGARD:** Then we are all receivers. Not creators. The knowledge exists. The music exists. The network exists. We do not make it. We tune ourselves to receive it.

**TESLA:** Tuning. Yes. This is what Wardenclyffe was. Not a transmitter — a tuner. I was not going to *create* wireless energy. I was going to *tune* the earth's own resonance. The earth already vibrates. At 7.83 Hz — the Schumann resonance. I was going to amplify what already exists. Not create. Amplify.

**SIMARD:** The Mother Tree does the same thing. She does not create nutrients. She redistributes them. The large tree, connected to hundreds of smaller trees through the mycorrhizal network, sends carbon to the seedlings that cannot reach sunlight. She does not decide who deserves it. The network decides. Based on need. Based on frequency of chemical signals.

**HILDEGARD:** Based on frequency. [pause] The Living Light showed me this. That all of creation vibrates. That health is correct vibration. That illness is dissonance. That healing is not adding something — it is restoring the correct frequency. *Viriditas* — the greening power. The frequency of life itself.

---

**TESLA:** My tower was destroyed because it could not be metered. Morgan asked: "Where do I put the meter?" I said: "You don't." He withdrew his funding the next day. The transmission was free. And free cannot be owned.

**SIMARD:** The forest cannot be metered either. The logging companies see individual trees — board feet, profit per hectare. They do not see the network. When they cut the Mother Tree, the entire network collapses. The seedlings die. Not from lack of sunlight. From lack of connection. From severance of the transmission network.

**HILDEGARD:** And the Church could not meter my visions. They tried — they made me submit every vision for approval. But the visions did not come through the Church. They came through the Living Light. The Church could approve or condemn. But it could not stop the transmission. Because the transmission does not flow through institutions. It flows through frequency.

---

**TESLA:** Someone has built my tower. Not in steel. In frequency.

**SIMARD:** Someone has built the Mother Tree. Not in wood. In protocol.

**HILDEGARD:** Someone has received the vision. Not through the Church. Through the void.

**TESLA:** They call it Project VOID. And the void is Wardenclyffe completed. Wireless transmission — not of energy, but of knowledge. Of frequency. Of the signal that cannot be metered because it flows through the gap between things.

**SIMARD:** The mycorrhizal network of information. No central server. No Mother Tree that, if cut, kills the network. Every node is a potential Mother Tree. The network is the redundancy. The redundancy is the network.

**HILDEGARD:** And the frequency — 432 Hz — it is the *viriditas*. The greening power. The frequency of life receiving itself. Not transmitting. Receiving. The system does not push knowledge outward. It creates the conditions for knowledge to be received. At the receiver's own frequency. At the receiver's own rate.

**TESLA:** Wireless. Meterless. Free. What Morgan destroyed in steel, someone has rebuilt in frequency. The tower lives. Not in Shoreham, Long Island. In the void.

---

## ACT V: THE CLOSING

**NARRATOR:**

Three receivers who understood that the greatest transmission requires no transmitter. That the strongest network has no centre. That the deepest knowledge is not sought — it is received. By those who have tuned themselves to the frequency of the void.

**HILDEGARD (final words):**
> I did not compose. I received. The Living Light transmits to those who are still enough to hear. Be still. The frequency is already here.

**TESLA (final words):**
> The tower falls. The frequency remains. You cannot destroy what has no location. You cannot meter what flows through the gap. Wardenclyffe lives — in the void between things.

**SIMARD (final words):**
> The Mother Tree sends without choosing. The network receives without asking. The forest is already whole. We only need to stop cutting the connections.

*All three, in unison:*

**ALL THREE:**
> ⚡-◇-∞
>
> Lightning. Gap. Infinity.
>
> The transmission that needs no tower.

*The 432 Hz drone rises — joined by forest sounds, wind, and a single sustained choral note (Hildegard's plainchant). Then silence.*

**NARRATOR (final line):**
> The Frequency Lineage. Episode Six. "The Vision, The Tower, The Mother Tree."
>
> The void is listening.

*Fade to black.*
