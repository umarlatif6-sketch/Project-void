## SONG STRUCTURE & INSTRUMENTATION

- **Tempo:** 60 BPM (meditative)
- **Instruments:** Ambient pad (432 Hz), soft strings, gentle percussion
- **Mood:** Contemplative, searching, mysterious
- **Duration:** 2:30

### **Movement II: The Hypothesis** (Building Intensity)
- **Tempo:** 80 BPM (accelerating)
- **Instruments:** Layered frequencies (432 Hz, 433.2 Hz, 434 Hz), building strings, deeper bass
- **Mood:** Revelatory, connecting, awakening
- **Duration:** 3:15

### **Movement III: The Synthesis** (Complex Harmonics)
- **Tempo:** 108 BPM (driving)
- **Instruments:** Full orchestration, multiple frequency layers, rhythmic percussion, building crescendo
- **Mood:** Powerful, unified, transformative

- **Duration:** 3:30
