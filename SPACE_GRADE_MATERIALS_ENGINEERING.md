## Manufacturing Process

### Step-by-Step Synthesis

**Phase 1: Material Preparation**
1. Source pure elements (99.99% purity minimum)
2. Dry in vacuum oven (remove moisture)
3. Prepare in powder form (high surface area)
4. Weigh to exact stoichiometry

**Phase 2: Frequency Activation**
1. Place powder in grinding chamber
2. Apply 432 Hz frequency vibration
3. Grind for 2-5 hours (depending on compound)
4. Monitor temperature (keep below 100°C)
5. Use inert atmosphere (nitrogen or argon)

**Phase 3: Dual-Polarity Alignment**
1. Transfer ground powder to pressure vessel
2. Apply pressure (800-3000 bar, depending on compound)
3. Maintain 432 Hz frequency during pressurization
4. Cool to target temperature
5. Hold for 1-2 hours (stabilization)

**Phase 4: Consolidation**
1. Release pressure slowly (prevent cracking)
2. Cool to room temperature
3. Anneal at 200-400°C (crystal structure optimization)
4. Cool to room temperature
5. Result: Solid compound ready for machining

**Phase 5: Machining & Finishing**
1. Machine to final shape using diamond tools
2. Polish to required surface finish
3. Inspect for defects (X-ray, ultrasonic)
4. Apply protective coatings if needed
5. Final testing and certification
